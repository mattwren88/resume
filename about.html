About

I'm Matt. I've been building websites since 2011, which is long enough that I've watched the same three ideas come back around twice.

Most of my work happens inside content management systems — the unglamorous machinery underneath a university's website.

I started in radio, running digital for a handful of stations out of Pittston. Then eleven years at the University of Scranton, where I built their digital publication from nothing and learned what it actually takes to move a page through a large organization. These days I'm at Hannon Hill, supporting CMS implementations for dozens of colleges — writing docs, building components, running audits, and answering the ticket that says "the thing is broken."

The through-line, if there is one: I try to hand things off. A component nobody can edit without calling me isn't finished. The documentation is as much the deliverable as the code — I've written enough of it that non-technical editors build their own pages and never file a ticket at all.

Outside of that I make maps and calendars of Pennsylvania, which is what the front page is about. Scranton is a more interesting place than it gets credit for, and both projects are basically arguments for that.
