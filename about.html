Experience Since 2011

I have been in web development since 2011. In my current role at Hannon Hill, I support web ecosystems for dozens of colleges and universities—writing technical documentation, troubleshooting CMS issues, building custom components, conducting performance audits, and managing full site integrations from design to deployment.

Recent projects include converting 20+ Figma designs into production-ready components, architecting 50+ custom CMS components with complete documentation, conducting technical audits covering 400+ pages, and building automated content systems with search and filtering capabilities.

I apply the same standards across all projects—structured workflows, measurable results, and code that's maintainable without ongoing developer dependency.
