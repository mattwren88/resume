# 3 SEO Basics Most Business Sites Miss

Many business websites look modern but still miss high-impact technical SEO basics. Here are three fixes I recommend first.

## 1. Write focused title tags and meta descriptions

Each page should target one intent and one primary topic. Keep title tags specific and avoid repeating the same copy across pages.

## 2. Improve heading structure

Use one clear H1 per page, then organize content with H2s and H3s. This helps both users and search engines understand your page.

## 3. Clean up internal links

Add internal links between related pages using clear anchor text. This improves crawl paths and helps users discover your most important content.

If you want a fast SEO tune-up, start here before chasing advanced tactics.
