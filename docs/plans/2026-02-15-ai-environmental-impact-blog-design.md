# Blog Post Design: AI Environmental Impact

## Purpose
Balanced myth-buster blog post that separates fact from misinformation about AI's environmental footprint. Aimed at small business owners wondering if using AI tools is irresponsible.

## Title
"Does Using AI Hurt the Environment? What the Data Actually Says"

## Structure

1. **Intro** - Headlines mix up using AI vs building AI. Let's look at what the data says.

2. **"A single AI query isn't the crisis you've heard about"** - IEA initially said 10x more than Google (2.9 Wh vs 0.3 Wh). Recent 2025 data: GPT-4o uses ~0.3 Wh, roughly comparable to Google search. Efficiency improving fast.

3. **"Training is where the real energy goes"** - GPT-4 training: ~50 GWh. One-time cost per model. DeepSeek-V3 achieved 95% lower training energy. Innovation is happening.

4. **"The water question"** - Data centers use significant water for cooling. Per-query estimates vary wildly (0.3 mL per OpenAI vs ~500 mL per UC Riverside depending on methodology). Transparency is improving but inconsistent.

5. **"Scale is the real conversation"** - Inference = 80-90% of total AI compute due to millions of daily users. The environmental focus should be on infrastructure and energy sources, not individual use.

6. **Closing** - Using AI for your work isn't the problem. The real impact is at the infrastructure level, and it's improving. Stay informed, use tools strategically.
