Zone 6a Corner Garden Master Plan

Location: Scranton, PA
USDA Zone: 6a (-10°F to -5°F average minimum)
Best Planting Windows: May–mid June | September–early October

Zone 6a Constraints

  • Last frost: ~May 10–15
  • First frost: ~Oct 10–15
  • Growing season: ~150 days
  • Common soil: compacted clay-loam
  • Freeze/thaw cycles and snow load matter
Always call 811 before digging, especially near alleyways.

Avoid planting canopy trees where a future garage or driveway may go.

Installation Steps

  1. Call 811.
  2. Define curved bed shape (~18–25 ft long).
  3. Remove sod fully.
  4. Loosen soil 8–12 inches deep.
  5. Blend 2–3 inches compost across entire bed.
  6. Place plants before digging holes.
  7. Plant trees with root flare slightly above grade.
  8. Install drip or soaker hose.
  9. Mulch 2–3 inches (not against trunks).
Most common mistake: planting trees too deep.

Watering Plan (Year 1)

  • Deep soak once per week
  • 10–15 gallons per tree
  • 5 gallons per shrub
  • Skip week if 1 inch rainfall
