🌿 Zone 6a Corner Garden Master Plan

Structured, native-forward landscape plan for Scranton, PA (USDA Zone 6a).
Hardiness: -10°F to -5°F

📍 Zone 6a Constraints

  • Last frost: ~May 10–15
  • First frost: ~Oct 10–15
  • Growing season: ~150 days
  • Common soil: compacted clay-loam
  • Freeze/thaw + snow load matter
⚠ Always call 811 before digging near alleyways.

🛠 Installation Steps

  1. Call 811.
  2. Define organic curved bed (~18–25 ft long).
  3. Remove sod completely.
  4. Loosen soil 8–12 inches.
  5. Blend 2–3 inches compost across entire bed.
  6. Dry-place plants before digging holes.
  7. Plant trees with root flare slightly above grade.
  8. Install drip/soaker hose.
  9. Mulch 2–3 inches (not touching trunks).
Most common mistake: planting trees too deep.

💧 Watering Plan (Year 1)

  • Deep soak once per week
  • 10–15 gallons per tree
  • 5 gallons per shrub
  • Skip if ~1 inch rainfall that week
